items:
  - q: Is PocketChomp really free?
    a: Yes. Barcode scanning, label scanning, search, voice and typed logging, every micronutrient, custom goals, custom foods and recipes, offline use and CSV export are all free, with no ads and no scan limit. PocketChomp+ is an optional subscription that adds AI photo logging, adaptive targets and longer-range insights. It never takes the basics away.
  - q: Do I need an account?
    a: No. You can use the entire app as a guest. Cloud sync is something you turn on, not a condition of using it, and your log is written to your phone first either way.
  - q: Does it work without a connection?
    a: Yes. Health Canada and USDA foods ship inside the app, and everything you have searched or logged before is cached on your device, so you can search, log and review with no connection at all.
  - q: How Canadian is the food database?
    a: More than 130,000 of the 200,000+ foods come from Canadian sources, including Health Canada's nutrient file and the house brands you find in the major Canadian grocery chains. Verified entries rank first in search, and the app is metric by default.
  - q: Is my food log private?
    a: Your log is stored on your device by default, and you can use the app without an account at all. If you turn on cloud sync, it is backed up to our server so it can follow you between devices. You can export everything to CSV or delete it, including the server copy, whenever you want. We do not sell your data.
  - q: Which phones does it run on?
    a: Android only for now. There is no iOS build yet. If you are on an iPhone, this is not the app for you today.
  - q: What does PocketChomp+ cost?
    a: $6.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial. It is optional, and the free tier is a complete logging app rather than a trial of a paid one.
