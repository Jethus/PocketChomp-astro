items:
  - q: Is PocketChomp really free?
    a: Yes. Barcode scanning, label scanning, search, typed meal descriptions, every micronutrient, custom goals, custom foods and recipes, offline use and CSV export are all free, with no ads and no scan limit. PocketChomp+ is an optional subscription that adds AI photo and voice logging, adaptive targets and longer-range insights. It never takes the basics away.
  - q: Do I need an account?
    a: No. You can use the entire app as a guest. Cloud sync is something you turn on, not a condition of using it, and your log is written to your phone first either way.
  - q: Does it work without a connection?
    a: Yes. Health Canada and USDA foods ship inside the app, and everything you have searched or logged before is cached on your device, so you can search, log and review with no connection at all.
  - q: How Canadian is the food database?
    a: More than {canadianFoods} of the {foods}+ foods come from Canadian sources, including Health Canada's nutrient file and the house brands you find in the major Canadian grocery chains. Verified entries rank first in search, and the app is metric by default.
  - q: Is my food log private?
    a: Your log is stored on your device by default, and you can use the app without an account at all. If you turn on cloud sync, it is backed up to our server so it can follow you between devices. You can export everything to CSV or delete it, including the server copy, whenever you want. We do not sell your data.
  - q: Which phones does it run on?
    a: Android, as an open beta on Google Play. An iOS version is planned. Join the email list and you'll hear when it's ready.
  - q: Does it work outside Canada?
    a: Yes. USDA foods ship inside the app next to Health Canada's, and barcode lookups fall back to Open Food Facts' worldwide database. Canadian groceries have the deepest coverage; US store brands are thinner. It will be on Google Play in the US too, priced in your local currency.
  - q: What does PocketChomp+ cost?
    a: $6.99 a month, or $39.99 a year with a free trial, extra long during the beta and launch. It is optional, and the free tier is a complete logging app rather than a trial of a paid one.
