## How this calculator picks your target

It estimates maintenance with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then subtracts the
daily deficit that matches your chosen pace (7,700 kcal per kilogram of body
weight, spread over the week). The result is a range, not a promise — your
true maintenance can sit anywhere in a ±10% band, so your deficit can too.

## Choosing a pace you'll actually keep

The biggest predictor of weight-loss success isn't the size of the deficit —
it's whether you're still running it in month three. A 0.25–0.5 kg/week pace
preserves muscle, keeps hunger manageable, and leaves room for real life. If
our numbers dip below a safe floor for your stats, we'll tell you to slow
down rather than hand you an unsustainable target.

## When the scale disagrees with the math

It will, at some point. Water, sodium, stress, and sleep all move daily weight
more than fat does. Judge your deficit on the two-week trend, not the daily
reading — and when you do adjust, change one small thing at a time. The
adjustment table above covers the common cases.
